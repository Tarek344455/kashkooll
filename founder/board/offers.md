# Offers lens
Score: 8 | Vote: FUND IF
## What is strong
- **Massive pain, proven spend**: Egyptian private tutoring market is ~$2B+/year, almost entirely cash/WhatsApp operated. Center owners lose money daily to no-shows, settlement disputes, cash leakage. Teachers lose income to admin overhead. Parents have zero visibility. The pain is acute and they *pay* for solutions (center management software exists but is clunky).
- **Buyers have money & are reachable**: Center owners run businesses with revenue. Teachers earn per session. Both are concentrated in known areas (cities, districts) and active on Facebook/WhatsApp/Instagram — reachable via direct sales, referrals, local ads.
- **Market growing**: Student population rising, private tutoring demand increasing, digital payment adoption accelerating (Instapay/Fawry), government pushing formalization.
- **Value equation scores high**: 
  - Dream outcome: "Run my center/teaching business on autopilot, zero cash leakage, parents love me" — 9/10
  - Perceived likelihood: Product *exists* (v2.5), not vaporware. Demo-able. — 8/10
  - Time to result: 0.3-sec check-in, 1-tap settlement = immediate. — 9/10
  - Effort/sacrifice: Switching cost is the main friction (data migration, staff training). — 5/10
- **Not competing on price**: Differentiation is *automation depth* (6 engines 24/7), *offline-first* (works in Egyptian connectivity), *Egyptian payment rails built-in*, *Space Glass UI* that students/parents love. This is a "feel stupid saying no" stack.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Switching cost / habit lock-in**: Centers run on WhatsApp/Excel/cash. Moving to an app requires behavior change from owner, secretary, teachers, students, parents *simultaneously*. If onboarding takes >1 week or breaks during exam season, they revert.
2. **Distribution to center owners**: No established channel. Cold-calling centers is slow. Referrals need first happy centers. Marketing budget unknown. CAC could exceed LTV if not careful.
3. **Payment rail dependency**: Instapay/Fawry/Vodafone Cash integration must be bulletproof. Any downtime = lost trust = churn. Regulatory changes (CBE, FRA) could break integrations.

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. What is the *exact* onboarding flow for a new center? How many days until first successful check-in + settlement? Can a non-technical owner do it alone?
2. What is your CAC today? How do the first 100 centers arrive? (Referrals? Sales team? Partnerships with governorates?)
3. What happens when Instapay/Fawry goes down for 4 hours during peak settlement (11 PM)? Show me the fallback.
4. What is the guarantee you can offer? "If settlement doesn't match cash, we pay the difference" — can the CFO model that?
5. Could you double the price and still be the obvious choice? What would need to be true?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- [ ] Onboarding time-to-value ≤ 3 days for a center (documented, tested)
- [ ] CAC < 3 months gross margin per center (modelled in CFO)
- [ ] Payment fallback documented and tested (offline ledger + retry queue)
- [ ] Guarantee defined, costed, and CFO-approved
- [ ] First 10 pilot centers signed with LOIs at target price
