# Kashkool (كشكول)

- What it is: Autonomous EdTech OS for the Egyptian market — a unified platform connecting centers, teachers, students, secretaries, and parents with AI-powered automation, offline-first architecture, and immutable financial ledgers.
- Who it is for: 
  - Primary buyers: Education center owners (مراكز الدروس الخصوصية) and independent teachers/tutors in Egypt
  - End users (free): Students (K-12, university), parents, center secretaries
- What it sells, at what price: 
  - B2B SaaS for centers: monthly subscription per center (price TBD via founder-pricing)
  - B2C SaaS for independent teachers: monthly subscription per teacher (price TBD)
  - Free for students/parents (B2B2C model)
  - Core value: 0.3-second NFC/QR check-in, 1-tap daily settlement, AI academic rescue, family wallet with Instapay/Vodafone Cash/Fawry, DRM-protected content, live Egypt heatmap for super-admin
- Where and how: 
  - Egypt nationwide (online + offline-first)
  - Mobile app (Flutter: iOS, Android, Windows) + Admin web dashboard
  - Distribution: direct sales to centers, teacher onboarding, app stores
  - Delivery: SaaS cloud (Supabase backend) + local Drift database for offline operation
- Budget and constraints: 
  - Existing codebase: Flutter 3.x, Supabase, Edge Functions, Spaceship AI Router (Port 4500)
  - Team: Technical team in place, building since 2024
  - Regulatory: Egyptian education market, data residency, payment integrations (Instapay, Fawry, Vodafone Cash)
  - Competitive: Local ERPs, generic LMS, WhatsApp-based operations
  - Key differentiator: Autonomous automation (6 background engines 24/7), Space Glass UI, offline-first, Egyptian payment rails
