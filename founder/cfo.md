## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: EGP 350,000 a month (Engineering team (6 engineers) EGP 180,000, Sales & onboarding team (4 people) EGP 80,000, Office & infrastructure EGP 25,000, Marketing & acquisition EGP 50,000, Legal, compliance, accounting EGP 15,000).

- **Break-even: 7 center-months a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (200 a day): **74%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 1000 a day.
