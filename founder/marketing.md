# Marketing Plan: Kashkool

**Objective**: 50 pilot centers in 30 days (Launch Pack), then 500 centers by month 6.

---

## 1. Positioning Statement

> **For** Egyptian tutoring center owners who lose money to manual operations and cash leakage,
> **Kashkool** is the **Autonomous EdTech OS** that runs your center while you sleep.
> **Unlike** ERPs (EduMe, SchoolPlus) that are cloud-only and manual,
> **Kashkool** works **offline-first**, settles in **1 tap**, and integrates **Egyptian payment rails natively**.

---

## 3. The Wedge: 6th October Governorate Pilot

**Why 6th October?**
- High density of tutoring centers (~500+)
- Tech-savvy owners (newer developments)
- Founder's network: 3 pilot LOIs already
- Contained geography = easier support

**Pilot Goal**: 10 centers live in 30 days, 50 in 90 days.
**Success Metric**: 80%+ trial-to-paid, <3% monthly churn, NPS > 50.

## 11. Red Lines (Stop Conditions)

- CAC > EGP 15,000 for 2 consecutive months → pause ads, fix funnel
- Trial-to-paid < 15% → fix onboarding/product, not marketing
- Monthly churn > 5% → fix product/support, not acquisition
- Pilot centers < 80% activated by Day 7 → fix onboarding process
