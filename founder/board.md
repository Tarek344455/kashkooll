## The Strongest Version of This Business (Board's View)

**Kashkool is not "software for tutoring centers." It is the *operating system* for the Egyptian private tutoring economy.**

The board sees a business that:
- **Owns the operations layer** — check-in, settlement, wallet, compliance — for a $2B+ market that runs on WhatsApp and cash
- **Has a genuine 10x technical moat** — offline-first Drift + 6 autonomous engines + Egyptian payment abstraction — that takes a competitor 12+ months to replicate
- **Can become the "Windows of Egyptian tutoring"** — if it wins distribution fast enough to trigger network effects (centers → teachers → students → parents → more centers → heatmap data → wallet liquidity)
- **Must say "no" ruthlessly** — to gradebooks, video libraries, parent chat — to stay the *OS*, not become a bloated LMS

**Where this differs from the founder's pitch:** The founder pitched features (Space Glass UI, AI router, DRM, heatmap). The board sees *one thing*: **autonomous center operations**. Every feature either serves that or gets cut. The pitch should be "Kashkool runs your center while you sleep" — not a feature list.
