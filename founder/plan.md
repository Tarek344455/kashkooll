## 1. Executive Summary

**Kashkool is the operating system for the Egyptian private tutoring economy.**

Not an LMS. Not a CRM. Not a payment app. **The OS that runs the center** — check-in, settlement, wallet, compliance, teacher tools, student engagement, super-admin radar — all autonomous, offline-first, integrated with Egyptian payment rails, wrapped in a Space Glass UI that feels like the future.

**The market**: $2B+ annually, 25M students, ~50K centers, 99% running on WhatsApp/Excel/cash.
**The product**: Built, production-ready (v2.5.0), 6 autonomous engines, Space Glass UI, offline-first Drift, Egyptian payments native.
**The moat**: 12-18 months before serious copycat (offline sync + autonomous engines + payment abstraction + operational secret).
**The risk**: Distribution and switching cost — entirely solvable with the Launch Pack.
