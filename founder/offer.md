## 2. The Guarantee (CFO-Costed)

**"First Settlement Match Guarantee"**

> "Run your first settlement on Kashkool. If the numbers don't match your cash count exactly, we refund your first 3 months — no questions asked."

- **CFO model**: <2% claim rate (based on immutable ledger + offline retry)
- **Cost per claim**: 3 × EGP 2,500 = EGP 7,500
- **Expected cost per center**: 0.02 × 7,500 = **EGP 150** (6% of month 1 revenue)
- **Funded from**: Marketing budget (replaces "trust" ad spend)
