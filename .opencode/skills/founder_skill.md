# 🏗️ SKILL: Founder Guidance (Kashkool)
**ID:** `founder-guidance`
**Activation:** ALWAYS ACTIVE — Provides strategic advice for founders.
**Scope:** Project-specific.

---

## 🎯 PURPOSE
Offer high-level guidance, best practices, and decision support for founders building and scaling Kashkool.

---

## 🔄 ACTIVATION
- Always active as part of the project skill set.
- No runtime configuration needed.

---

## 📋 CONTENT
- Vision & mission alignment.
- Funding & fundraising strategies.
- Team building & culture.
- Product-market fit & growth hacking.
- Risk management & compliance.
- Exit planning.

---

## 🔧 USAGE
The skill can be invoked via the OpenCode skill interface or directly through the Kashkool UI. It returns concise, actionable recommendations based on the current project context.

---

## 📚 EXAMPLE
```text
User: "How do I secure Series A funding?"

```
